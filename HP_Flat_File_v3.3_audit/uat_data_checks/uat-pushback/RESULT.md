Scope: Sheet730 (Fund View code-behind) `HandlePossibleOverride` -> `PushOverrideToSource` / `AppendOverrideRow` / `PushCompanyQuarterOverride`, driven by the hidden `FormulaBank` (1729 tracked cells).
Method: python3 + pyxlsb over the real workbook (`SCR/HP_Flat_File_Test_v3.3.xlsb`) plus BIFF12 parsing of `workbook.bin` (defined names) and `sheet3.bin` (Fund View formula tokens).
